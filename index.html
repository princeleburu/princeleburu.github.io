01 / Biography

Education as a space
of possibility.

I am an education professional and researcher with two decades of experience in South African private higher education. As a Programme Manager in Commerce at Rosebank International, my work centres on academic leadership and the everyday conditions that help lecturers and students flourish.

My doctoral research at the University of Cape Town examines curriculum practices in South Africa’s private higher education institutions. Through Critical Realism and decolonial scholarship, I explore how knowledge acquires authority, and how educators can open space for more just educational possibilities.

I bring these questions into my work with colleagues and emerging researchers. I am particularly interested in the relationship between institutional conditions and lecturer agency, and in what meaningful curriculum transformation looks like in practice.
